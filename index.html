About Me

Some students have a subject they love. Mine is motion itself — the sprint, the slope, the water, the wall.

Ever since elementary school, if there was a ball, a board, a bike or a bow within reach, my hands found it first. What started as endless energy has become something more deliberate: a habit of walking into any sport as a beginner, and staying until it feels like mine. Land, water, snow, ice — each one taught me a different muscle, and the same lesson. Show up. Fall down. Go again.

In Fall 2027 I hope to bring that energy to a new classroom — and a new field, pool, and gym — as a Grade 9 student. [저희 아이 소개 문구를 여기에 추가로 넣으셔도 됩니다]
